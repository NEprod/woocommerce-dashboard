Shopify native mixed-basket conditions and safe alternatives

Shopify documents native packed-size box selection, but only for saved Boxes. A non-Box default, any missing item size, no fitting box or platform packing limits cause fallback to the default. TLC's audited default is an Envelope; its largest Box is 35 cm long, shorter than flat A3. Individual solo assignments do not solve this mixed basket.

A separately approved native trial would need accurate packed sizes for every item, a measured A3-capable Box and a Box default, then an actual Royal Mail checkout test. No guarantee of TLC constraints or multi-parcel splitting is claimed. A custom planner callback needs eligible carrier-calculated shipping, approved permissions/rates and an isolated public HTTPS service. Basic cannot register this custom rate path. Weight-based/profile proposals remain manual and cannot prove geometry; no shipping changes are applied here.
