TLCDashboard publishing capabilities

Application: up to 250 original gallery images for new products, staged in batches of 20, with complete media identity/order readback. Existing 10 MiB per-file, 30 MiB total and evidence/time budgets still apply. Nothing is truncated. Unsupported formats or incomplete readback block publication.

For existing products, unchanged images are retained; reviewed alternate-text and order changes preserve their media IDs. Adding/removing/replacing existing product images remains blocked: this publisher does not blindly rebuild a gallery.

Application variant review/readback supports 32 active variants. Shopify's current product model supports up to 2,048 variants, but that is not a claim that TLCDashboard supports that many. Remote-only variants are protected; destructive list replacement is not performed.
