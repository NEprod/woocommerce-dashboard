Uplift rule and live-price safety

Etsy starts from the current/chosen Shopify price, or its rounded suggestion if no price is entered. Add the larger of the percentage or minimum uplift, then round upwards. Prices already ending in .49/.99 stay there. Suggestions do not change live product prices.

Sales channel fees — Etsy

Editable UK starting assumptions, not live Etsy charges. Shopify continues to use Payment fee profiles/defaults below. Uplift is not a selling fee.

Fee bases and limitations

Per-item estimates assume one item per order, item price only, no postage/gift wrap/buyer taxes. Listing allowance is approximate GBP; offsite advertising is conditional, so enable its rate only for the scenario you want to model. VAT applies only to the checked fees; set your actual treatment, including zero where appropriate.
