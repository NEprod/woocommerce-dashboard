Cost assumptions and interpretation
    {% for item in estimate.assumptions %}
  • {{ item }}
    • {% endfor %}

For each tier, the bounds use the lowest- and highest-cost eligible variant repeated to the tier quantity, matching the existing allowance for identical units. If the normal price of a set is below its offer price, Shopify must not increase the payable amount; a cost bound for that set is not a guaranteed discounted checkout outcome. A quantity-tier's repeat setting controls multiple sets per cart, not whether an individual variant may repeat.

Markup is contribution ÷ product cost; margin is contribution ÷ offer price. These are distinct and exclude costs outside the product cost-per-item.
